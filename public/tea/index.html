How to play Tiến lên

Select one card, a pair, triple, four of a kind, a straight of at least 3 cards, or 3–4 consecutive pairs. Straights and consecutive pairs cannot include 2. Beat a set with the same kind and length; ranks run 3 to 2, suits ♠ < ♣ < ♦ < ♥.

3 consecutive pairs beat one 2. Four of a kind beat one or two 2s and 3 consecutive pairs. 4 consecutive pairs also beat four of a kind and may interrupt another player's turn, even after passing. Stronger combinations of the same kind beat weaker ones.

The smallest dealt card opens the first game; the previous winner opens a rematch. Passing locks you out until the set clears, except for a 4-pair interruption. Each turn is 30 seconds: timeout passes, or leads the smallest card. First out wins. No instant wins, penalties, betting or prizes.

How to play Bài cào

2–8 players, three cards each. Only you can see your cards until everyone is ready or the 30-second timer ends.

A is 1 point; 2–9 are their face value; 10, J, Q and K are 0. Add the points and keep the last digit. Three J/Q/K cards are “ba tây” and beat 9 points.

Equal points tie. Suits do not break ties. This version has no special triples or liêng, no betting and no prizes.

Texas Hold’em rules & hand ranks

2–8 players. Each new round starts with 1,000 virtual chips per player; blinds are 10/20. Play chips are separate from your wallet, cannot be bought or redeemed and reset next round.

You get two private cards. Five shared cards appear through preflop, flop (3), turn (1) and river (1). Make your best five-card hand using any combination of the seven cards.

Check if no call is due, call the required amount, raise, go all in or fold. “Raise to” means your total bet for this street, including chips already placed. Each turn is 30 seconds; timeout checks when possible, otherwise folds.
